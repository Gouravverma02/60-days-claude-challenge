# Day 4 - Personalized Career Roadmap with Claude

## Objective
Generate a personalized career roadmap using Claude based on current skills, goals, and timeline.

## My Inputs
- Current Situation: Student
- Skills: Java, Front-End Development, Cybersecurity Preparation
- Goal: Get a Job
- Timeline: 6 Months

## Key Learnings
- Claude analyzed my current position before creating recommendations.
- It identified skill gaps in DSA, SQL, Spring Boot, and Interview Preparation.
- It suggested a structured learning path with monthly milestones.
- It recommended practical projects and networking strategies.

## What Surprised Me Most
Claude did not simply answer the goal. It created a complete roadmap by connecting my strengths, weaknesses, projects, networking strategy, and job application plan into one structured career path.

## Deliverables
- Personalized Career Roadmap
- LinkedIn Post
- Roadmap Infographic
