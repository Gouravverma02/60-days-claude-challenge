---

## Biggest Learning

The best results come from choosing the right combination of model and reasoning effort rather than always using the most powerful model.

- Haiku = Speed
- Sonnet = Daily productivity
- Opus = Deep thinking

Reasoning effort should match task complexity:
- Low → Quick tasks
- Standard → Daily work
- High → Difficult problems
- Max → Deep research
