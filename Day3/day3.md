# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Objective
Understand how assigning different roles/personas affects Claude's responses.

## Question Asked
"What are the best strategies for launching a new product?"

## Persona 1: Marketing Consultant

### Response Summary
- Focused on branding
- Discussed customer pain points
- Emphasized storytelling and engagement
- Suggested marketing campaigns

## Persona 2: Data Analyst

### Response Summary
- Focused on KPIs and metrics
- Recommended market research
- Discussed data-driven decision making
- Suggested performance tracking

## Biggest Observation

The same question produced significantly different responses based on the assigned role. The Marketing Consultant emphasized persuasion and customer engagement, while the Data Analyst prioritized measurable outcomes and evidence-based strategies.

## Key Learnings

1. Role-based prompting improves response relevance.
2. Different personas prioritize different information.
3. Clear role assignment leads to more specialized outputs.
4. Small prompt changes can dramatically improve AI responses.
